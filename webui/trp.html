How a mapping works
The server listens on the public port. On each connection it asks the device to open a socket to the local service, then pipes the two together.
Someone connects to
server:4444
Server forwards over tun to
device 127.0.0.1:8022
The device must be online for a mapping to work, and the public port has to be allowed through the server firewall.
